Graduation & Senior Pictures in St. Paul

You worked hard for this. Celebrate it with graduation pictures that feel like you, whether that's cap and gown on campus, your favorite jersey on the field, or just you, somewhere you love.

Senior pictures and cap-and-gown photos

Whether you're a high school senior or finishing college, your graduation session is all about you: your style, your interests and what's next. We'll mix classic portraits (the ones for graduation announcements and Grandma's fridge) with relaxed, candid shots that show your personality.

A one-hour session leaves time for a couple of locations or outfit changes. Bring your cap and gown, letter jacket, instrument, team gear, or anything else that tells your story. Friends, siblings and pets are welcome to join for a few shots.

When to schedule graduation pictures

  • High school senior pictures: many seniors book in late summer or fall of senior year, when the weather's nice. Check your school's yearbook deadline when you plan.
  • Cap and gown photos: spring, once your gown arrives, is perfect for graduation announcements and grad party displays.
  • Fall color: a fall senior session in the Twin Cities is hard to beat. See fall photo sessions.

Where to take graduation photos in the Twin Cities

Your school or campus is a natural choice. Around St. Paul, local favorites also include Como Park, Summit Avenue, downtown St. Paul and the Mississippi River bluffs, or anywhere that means something to you.

I photograph graduates from schools all around St. Paul and the Twin Cities, including Roseville, Maplewood, Woodbury, Cottage Grove, Lake Elmo, Afton, Eagan and Edina.

Good to know

Graduation photo questions

How many outfits can I bring?

Two or three outfits fit comfortably in a one-hour session. Bring one more casual look and one dressier look, plus your cap and gown if you have it.

Can friends join my graduation session?

Yes. A few friends or teammates can join for some fun group shots. Mention who's coming when you book so we can plan the time.

Will my photos be ready for graduation announcements?

Let me know your announcement or yearbook deadline when you book, and we'll plan your session date around it.
