Fall Family Photo Shoots in the Twin Cities

Crunchy leaves, cozy sweaters and that warm golden light. Fall is the most popular season for family pictures in Minnesota, and for good reason. Let's capture your family surrounded by color, just in time for holiday cards.

When to book fall family photos

Fall color in the Twin Cities usually peaks somewhere between late September and mid-October, depending on the year and the trees. The weeks around peak color book up quickly, so it's best to reserve your date in late summer or as early as you can.

The best light comes during golden hour, roughly the last hour before sunset. In fall the sun sets a little earlier each week, so evening sessions start earlier too. That's good news for little ones' bedtimes.

Where to take fall photos around St. Paul

Wooded trails, fields of tall grass and tree-lined streets all glow in autumn. Around St. Paul, local favorites include Como Park, Hidden Falls Regional Park, the Mississippi River bluffs and the tree-lined boulevard of Summit Avenue. If your own backyard or neighborhood trees are putting on a show, that works beautifully too.

I photograph families all around St. Paul and the Twin Cities, including Roseville, Maplewood, Woodbury, Cottage Grove, Lake Elmo, Afton, Eagan and Edina.

What to wear for fall family pictures

  • Warm, earthy colors. Rust, mustard, olive, cream, camel and denim look beautiful against fall leaves. Skip neon colors and big logos.
  • Texture and layers. Knits, flannel, corduroy and scarves add depth to your photos and keep everyone warm on chilly Minnesota evenings.
  • Comfortable shoes. We may walk through leaves, grass or trails.
  • Jackets for between shots. Bring warm layers to throw on between photos so nobody ends up shivering.

Fall photo session questions

When is the best time for fall family photos in Minnesota?

Peak color in the Twin Cities usually falls between late September and mid-October. Book early for those weeks, and plan for an evening session during golden hour for the warmest light.

Can we use our fall photos for holiday cards?

Yes! Fall sessions are perfect for holiday cards. Booking in September or early October leaves plenty of time to choose your favorites and order cards.

What if the weather doesn't cooperate?

Minnesota fall weather can change fast. If rain or a cold snap rolls in on your session day, we'll talk about moving to a better day.
